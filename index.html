Privacy Policy for Copy Current URL

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Copy Current URL is a Microsoft Edge extension that allows users to copy the URL of the currently active tab to their clipboard.

Information We Access

When you use the extension, it temporarily accesses the URL of the currently active Microsoft Edge tab. This information is used only to copy the URL to your clipboard at your request.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your browsing history, tab URLs, personal information, or clipboard contents.

How Information Is Used

The active tab URL is used only for the extension's single purpose: copying that URL to your clipboard. The URL is processed locally in your browser and is not sent to our servers or any third party.

Notifications

The extension may display a toast notification to confirm that the URL was copied successfully. These notifications do not contain or transmit personal information.

Third-Party Services

The extension does not use analytics, advertising services, tracking technologies, remote code, or third-party data-processing services.

Data Security

Because the extension does not transmit or store user data, no user data is maintained on our servers.

Children's Privacy

The extension does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the extension's functionality or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised update date.
